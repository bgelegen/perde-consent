Frequently asked questions

What marketing, engineering and legal teams ask us most.

Book a demo
Will more visitors accept the banner?

Banners that appear immediately, speak the visitor's language and match your brand get answered more often than ones that arrive late from another server. Perde gives Accept and Reject equal weight by default so the consent you collect holds up, and the dashboard shows how choices change by region and policy after every update.

Will it slow my site down?

No. The banner ships inside your own bundle, so there is no extra script to download and nothing waits on a third-party server before it can render.

Can we make it match our brand?

Yes. Use theme tokens for colours, type and radius, override props, rearrange the parts, or build a fully custom UI on the same consent state.

Does it show the right banner in each country?

Perde applies opt-in rules for the EU, UK and Türkiye, opt-out rules for California, Québec's Law 25, and simpler notices elsewhere. Some visitors may see no banner at all when the law doesn't call for one.

How do we prove we are compliant?

Every choice is stored as a consent record with the region, policy version and time. Code and website audits keep a trail of what changed, who reviewed it and how it was resolved.

Who is Perde for?

Product and engineering teams that ship often and want privacy checks inside their normal workflow, plus the legal and marketing teams who rely on that work.

How is Perde different from compliance automation tools?

Compliance automation tools collect policies and attestations. Perde looks at what your code and website actually do, and catches privacy problems before they reach customers.

Does Perde give legal advice?

No. Perde gives you evidence and suggested fixes. Decisions about your legal obligations belong with your own counsel.
